Our Service

What makes the journey easy

The small things we take care of, so the ride from Palanpur to Mumbai stays comfortable both ways.

Best for your journey

Sleeper berths, a steady highway route and a driver who knows every turn of NH48 — built around a good night's travel, not just a ticket.

Clean buses

Every bus is swept, wiped down and checked before it leaves the depot, so your berth and blanket are ready before you are.

Healthy food at dinner

An overnight halt at a trusted, hygienic hotel for dinner along the way, so you eat well before settling in for the night.

On time, both ways

Fixed evening departures and morning arrivals on both routes, so you can plan the rest of your day around a time you can trust.

Our Routes

Two directions, one highway

Both routes run the same towns along NH48 through Gujarat and Maharashtra, in reverse order to each other.
